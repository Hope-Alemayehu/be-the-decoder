How to play

Hidden bit-flip errors have hit the data qubits, the edges of this lattice. The glowing nodes are defects: they sit at the ends of error chains. Click edges to flip them until no defect is lit, joining defects to each other or to a boundary, then press Submit.

You win if what is left over does not connect the left boundary to the right boundary, which would be a logical error. The reveal then shows the real errors and how a minimum-weight perfect matching decoder did on the same round.
